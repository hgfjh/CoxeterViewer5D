# Exact Data And Drawing Conventions

The same scene can contain exact combinatorics and invented coordinates. The
viewer names them separately.

## Source Or Certified Data

These claims come from imported source data or stored artifacts with explicit
scope:

- Coxeter matrix entries and source references;
- exact-backend Cayley balls within their stated radius;
- Gram/signature and interval-geometry artifacts;
- finite-action, torsion-free, or external-checker certificates when supplied.

One certificate does not promote unrelated claims. In particular, a valid
finite action does not prove torsion-freeness.

An automatic cover-discovery certificate is narrower than Selberg's existence
theorem and stronger than action validation. It records a particular subgroup,
a complete prime-order torsion list, and the absence of fixed points in that
coset action. A bounded search that found nothing is a search report, not a
torsion-free-cover certificate.

## Exact In-Repo Incidence

For accepted finite input, the app deterministically checks:

- directed generator lifts and lifted attaching maps in `hat X`;
- bigon, edge, and relation-cell fibers of `hat X -> bar X`;
- signed compressed boundaries;
- abstract walls generated by opposite sides;
- wall parity, embeddedness, two-sidedness, and self-osculation witnesses;
- cooriented edge directions;
- lawful-cell sign words, sources, sinks, and positive paths;
- ascending and descending link incidence;
- a proven optimum when exact search finishes with equal bounds.

For a discovered cover, the external artifact also carries the subgroup and
torsion evidence. For a virtual algebraic-fibering run, the algebraic record
must additionally include `H -> Z`, its relator sums, image/primitivity check,
and the hypotheses used to infer anything about its kernel.

These are finite combinatorial results, not coordinates.

## Browser Diagnostics

The label **browser diagnostic** means the calculation is deterministic and
inspectable but is not an external theorem certificate. Typical examples are
wall pathology checks, finite link connectivity, and a completed local search.

The paper's incoherence and Morse conclusions require additional hypotheses.
Passing the finite checks does not establish those hypotheses automatically.

## Drawings

The following never change the mathematical complex:

- shell or force-relaxed node positions;
- parallel offsets used to separate the two directed edges shared by a pair of
  lifted bigons;
- straight wall arcs between edge midpoints;
- lifted, glass, or outline-only relation surfaces;
- ghost context and visibility filters;
- label lanes, camera pose, and screenshot framing;
- Klein, Poincare, axes, or PCA reduction to the displayed 3D scene.

Drawing-only nodes and edges carry explicit flags and source IDs. Exported
research data uses the underlying cellular records, not the mesh coordinates.

## How To Read A Claim

Ask four questions:

1. Which layer is active: Davis, `hat X`, `bar X`, Gamma, or Projection?
2. Is the statement about incidence, a finite browser check, or coordinates?
3. Which source hash and certificate scope support it?
4. Does a theorem require hypotheses not checked by this artifact?

The Focus Inspector answers the first two. Research status and the exported
experiment bundle answer the remaining two.
